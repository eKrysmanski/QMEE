Description of columns

athal_genotype = Arabidopsis thaliana genotype, Col-0 is wild-type
treatment = Treatment infiltrated into primary leaf of 3.5 week old plants
group = Arbitrary index to help group the mock-treatment with respective SAR-treatments
induction = Mock or SAR induced
population = Bacterial level in distant leaves at 72 hours post inoculation in colony forming units per leaf disks (cfu/ld)
